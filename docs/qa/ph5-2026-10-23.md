## Decisions and privacy

See [PH5 specification](../product/ph5-spec.md). A confirmed legacy manual ledger is not silently counted again: start advanced calculations with a new contract version. Advanced contracts reject new writes through the old manual ledger endpoint. Existing financial records remain available. New financial exports are scoped to the account owner; retained evidence uses the existing anonymized financial-retention policy. No new private-media cache or provider credential is introduced.

Plans and bank behavior remain explicit evaluation settings: no real billing or outbound bank API is activated by this code. A configured key alone cannot turn on real sales. The manual payout option records a human-supplied reference; it does not transfer money.

## Short manual test

Use ordinary local test accounts with the respective roles; the deleted production example accounts are not restored.

1. Owner: Business → Plans, branches & payouts → Business plan. Choose Advanced, inspect the amount/limits, explicitly accept terms and activate the zero-price simulated plan. Failed/unknown results must not enable new capabilities; reconcile unknown results before another attempt.
2. Business: create a fresh coach contract version in existing coach management. In Advanced → Contracts, select its percentage, per-session or fixed calculation. Percentage follows the signed gross/net share. Unlimited memberships require a per-session or fixed basis.
3. Complete past reservations, then close a past period. Approve the statement. Coach → Finance → Plans, branches & payouts shows only the coach's own lines. Open a dispute; authorized business staff/admin resolves it with a reason and, when needed, a signed adjustment.
4. Record an unknown simulated payout. A second payout must be blocked. Reconcile it as failed, then retry with a new reference; reconcile succeeded results without another debit. Keep the receipt and exercise a post-payment refund/correction.
5. Before a membership's first use, select allowed services across two branches and allocate exactly 10,000 basis points. Consume the same final session from both branches concurrently: one succeeds. Other organizations/unselected services remain denied.
6. Narrow dates/branches in the bottom filter sheet; compare gross sales, verified collections, distinct members, capacity, renewal membership orders and allocated net proceeds. Paging the statement list must preserve the full-scope summary balance.
7. Downgrade to Basic or stop a branch's new sales. Existing records and bookings remain; excess-branch new service versions and new advanced configurations are restricted. Previously approved payouts remain accessible.
